Glacier Discharge Alerts

Subscribe below — gate number, percentage and email
NEW · v5.5
Enter a glacier gate number, an increase threshold (%) and your email address. An alert is sent when a newly available, completed month's discharge is at least that percentage higher than the most recent earlier same calendar month with valid data. If last year's observation is unavailable or insufficient, the system looks further back. Only increases trigger alerts.

We require at least 80% reported data coverage, and the reference discharge must be at least 0.10 Gt/yr. After signing up, confirm your subscription by opening the emailed Google Form and pressing Submit.
